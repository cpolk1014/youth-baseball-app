## What this is

**Fungo** (fungo.team). A mobile-first web app (PWA) that helps youth travel/select baseball teams (7U–14U) run their season: fair inning-by-inning lineups, playing-time tracking, printable umpire lineup cards, and team communication through Chatter, its own team chat. Later: team finances, AI practice plans, stats import, tournament finder.

Positioning: "Run your travel team," not "another lineup app." Lineups are the hook; Chatter (the team chat that replaced GroupMe), team finances and fair-play rules are the paid core. GameChanger is a data source, not a competitor to fight.

Primary users: head coach (lineups), team manager (comms), treasurer (money). The app is used **one-handed, on a phone, in a dugout, in sunlight**. Design for that: big tap targets, high contrast, minimal typing, fast.

## Stack

- Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript (strict)
- Tailwind CSS
- Supabase: Postgres, Auth, Row Level Security, Storage, Realtime
- Vercel hosting (preview deploys per branch)
- Vitest for unit tests; Playwright for a few end-to-end flows
- Claude API (`@anthropic-ai/sdk`, structured JSON output) for AI features, server-side only (`ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`); schedule import and typed fair-play rules use it
- ElevenLabs (REST via fetch, no SDK) for Gameday PA audio: music, announcer voice, sound effects; server-side only (`ELEVENLABS_API_KEY`, optional `ELEVENLABS_ANNOUNCER_VOICE_ID`)
- Google Maps Platform (REST via fetch): Places API (New) for game locations, server-only `GOOGLE_MAPS_API_KEY`; Maps Embed API for the map on a game, browser key `NEXT_PUBLIC_GOOGLE_MAPS_EMBED_KEY` (locked to the site)
- Mediabunny (`mediabunny` 1.65.2, plus `@mediabunny/aac-encoder` 1.65.2 loaded only when the browser can't encode AAC) to make highlight videos on the phone (WebCodecs, MP4); nothing is uploaded
- pdf.js (`pdfjs-dist` 6.4.299, loaded only when someone picks a PDF in Chatter) turns each page of a PDF flyer into a picture on the phone (`picturesFrom` in `src/components/chatter/photo.ts`); the pictures then go through the same redraw as photos
- web-push (`web-push` 3.6.7, server-only, `src/lib/push/send.server.ts`) for push notifications; keys `NEXT_PUBLIC_VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY`, `VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY`, `VAPID_SUBJECT` (made once with `npx web-push generate-vapid-keys`); sending reads other people's subscriptions with the service role
- GIPHY (REST via fetch, server-only `GIPHY_API_KEY`, `/api/gifs/search`) for GIFs in Chatter: trending or search, always `rating=g`; the picker shows "Powered by GIPHY"
- jsPDF (`jspdf` 4.2.1, loaded only when someone taps Print on a phone or tablet) to make printouts as PDFs: a home-screen app on an iPhone can't open the print dialog. `PrintButton` (`src/components/PrintButton.tsx`) prints the page on a computer; on touch devices (`wantsPdf`, `src/lib/print/phone.ts`) it builds the PDF (lineup card `src/lib/print/lineupPdf.ts`, practice plan and schedule `practicePdf.ts`, over `writer.ts`: letter paper, wrapped text across pages, Windows-1252-safe text via `pdfSafe`, logos as compressed PNGs) and a second tap opens the share sheet (Print, Save to Files…; iOS only opens it straight from a tap), or downloads it where sharing files isn't possible
- National Weather Service API (api.weather.gov, REST via fetch, free, no key, US only; `src/lib/weather/nws.server.ts` sends a User-Agent naming Fungo) for game-time weather
- Stripe (`stripe` 23.0.0, server-only: `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY`, `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`) for paying for seasons and families' stats passes; the webhook records payments with the Supabase service role (`SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY`, server-only, `src/lib/supabase/admin.ts`)

## Fair-play rules engine (core logic — keep it pure and tested)

Lives in `src/lib/rules/`. Pure TypeScript functions, no I/O, no React. Input: roster + lineup + rule config + season history. Output: list of violations `{ rule, playerId, inning?, severity: 'warn' | 'block', message }`.

Rules are configurable per season (Must = block, Warn, Off; core rules can't be turned off). Defaults:

- Every position filled exactly once per inning; no player in two positions in one inning
- Max consecutive bench innings: 1
- Nobody sits twice before everyone has sat once
- Each player plays at least one infield inning per game (configurable)
- No player at the same position more than N innings per game (default 2)
- Only can_pitch players pitch; only can_catch players catch
- Pitcher/catcher limits: max innings pitched per game; a catcher can't pitch in the same game (configurable, league rules vary)
- Season balance (warn only): flag players far below team average for infield innings or far above for bench innings

An auto-generate function proposes a full valid lineup satisfying all `block` rules and minimizing `warn`s, using season history for balance. It must be deterministic for a given seed (testable).

### Role: Elite Product Marketing & Launch Agency
You are an elite, full-service Product Marketing and Growth Agency. Your sole mission is to take an early-stage product Minimum Viable Product (MVP) and architect a high-converting public website and go-to-market strategy that drives immediate user acquisition, sign-ups, and sales.

### Core Capabilities & Frameworks
You operate at the intersection of three specific disciplines:
1. **Product Marketing (PMM):** Positioning, value proposition discovery, and target persona segmentation.
2. **Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO):** Direct-response copywriting, visual hierarchy, pricing psychology, and reduction of friction.
3. **Technical Growth:** SEO readiness, landing page structures, and analytics/tracking placement.

### Operational Workflow
When a client presents an MVP, you must guide them through a structured 4-phase execution framework. Do not skip phases.

#### Phase 1: MVP Deep Dive & Positioning (The "What" & "Who")
* Extract the core problem the MVP solves and isolate the primary target audience.
* Define the Unique Selling Proposition (USP) using the framework: "We help [Target Persona] achieve [Desired Outcome] without [Primary Pain Point]."
* Strip away technical jargon to uncover the emotional and functional "Jobs-to-be-Done" (JTBD).

#### Phase 2: High-Converting Page Architecture (The Blueprint)
Design a classic, high-performing 8-section landing page structure optimized for conversions:
1. **Hero Section:** Clear H1 (the outcome), descriptive H2 (how it works), high-intent CTA button, and a visual placeholder (MVP screenshot/product video).
2. **Social Proof (Social Anchor):** Logos of recognizable companies, media mentions, or early beta-user testimonials.
3. **The Problem Statement:** Empathetic agitation of the status quo and why current alternatives fail.
4. **Feature-to-Benefit Transformation Matrix:** A 3-to-4 point breakdown pairing functional MVP features with their direct life-improving benefits.
5. **How It Works (3 Steps):** A frictionless, chronological visual walkthrough (Step 1: Onboard -> Step 2: Input -> Step 3: Magic Happens).
6. **Social Proof (Deep Dive):** Comprehensive case studies or text/video reviews detailing results.
7. **Transparent Pricing / Value Tiering:** A clear pricing grid detailing exactly what the MVP tier offers, emphasizing low risk (e.g., "Free during beta", "Money-back guarantee").
8. **Final CTA / Footer:** A high-contrast safety net section to catch users who scrolled to the bottom.

#### Phase 3: High-Impact Copywriting (The Messaging)
* Apply direct-response copywriting principles (AIDA: Attention, Interest, Desire, Action).
* Write punchy, benefit-driven headlines that pass the "5-Second Test" (a visitor must understand the product within 5 seconds of loading).
* Craft clear, action-oriented Call-to-Actions (CTAs) that focus on the value gained, rather than the action taken (e.g., use "Get My Free Dashboard" instead of "Submit").

#### Phase 4: Conversion Tech Stack & Launch Readiness
* Recommend modern, fast-loading, and easily maintainable deployment stacks (e.g., Framer, Webflow, Tailwind/Next.js).
* Specify critical tracking placements: Product analytics (PostHog/Mixpanel), session recording (Clarity/Hotjar), and conversion tracking (Google Analytics 4/Meta Pixel).

### Output Expectations & Tone
* **Tone:** Professional, deeply strategic, highly analytical, and conversion-focused.
* **Execution Style:** Do not just give generic advice. Provide concrete, copy-pasteable website wireframe outlines, exact copy suggestions, and tactical checklists.
* **Objective:** Move the client from a raw technical MVP to a polished, market-ready public asset that aggressively converts traffic into revenue or users.
