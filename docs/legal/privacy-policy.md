RallyCap ("RallyCap," "we," "us") is a web app that helps youth travel and select baseball teams run their season: lineups, schedules, team money, team communication, tournaments, and players' own stats. RallyCap is operated by [legal entity name], [mailing address].

Youth baseball means kids, so we built RallyCap to collect as little as we can about them, to keep it inside their team and family, and never to sell it. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, who helps us run RallyCap, and the choices you have.

If you have questions, email **[privacy@rallycap.team]**.

---

## 1. Who this policy covers

- **Adults who use RallyCap:** coaches, team managers, treasurers, parents and guardians, and club directors and admins. You must be 18 or older to have a RallyCap account.
- **Players (kids).** Players don't have accounts and don't sign in. Information about a player is added by an adult on their team: a coach adds them to the roster, and a parent or guardian may add their own child's stats and walk-up song.
- **Visitors** to our public website.

## 2. The short version

- **Kids:** we collect only what a team needs to run its season. That means a first and last name (or however the coach enters it), a jersey number, positions, and lineups and playing time. We never ask for a player's email, phone, address, school, birthday or photo.
- **Team information stays with the team.** Only people the coach invites can see it, and different roles see different things.
- **A player's own stats stay with their family.** Coaches can't see the box scores parents import.
- **We don't sell personal information,** we don't show ads, and we don't use tracking or advertising cookies in the app.
- **Some features use trusted service providers** (listed in section 6), such as AI that reads a screenshot you upload. They may use your data only to provide that service to us.
- **You can see, correct, export or delete** your information. Email us, or use the controls in the app.

## 3. What we collect

### 3.1 Information about you (adults)

| What | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Email address** | When you sign in | Your account; sign-in links and codes; important notices (e.g. a payment receipt or a change to these terms) |
| **Name** (as you'd like teammates to see it) | Optional, on My profile or when you join a team | So your team knows who you are |
| **Cell phone number** | Optional, on My profile | Shown to people on your teams (Coaches & parents, and the roster next to your child) so they can call you |
| **Venmo handle** | Optional, on My profile | Never shown to anyone else. Teammates get a "Pay with Venmo" button that takes them to Venmo without seeing your handle |
| **Your role on each team** (owner, coach, manager, treasurer, parent) | When you create or join a team | What you can see and do |
| **Which players are your children** | When you link yourself to a player | So you see your child's stats, money and homework, and can set up their walk-up song |
| **Purchases** | When you buy something | Your purchase history and what you've unlocked (see 3.5) |

### 3.2 Information about players (kids)

Added by the team's coaches, managers or the player's parents or guardians:

- **Roster details:** name, jersey number, preferred positions, whether they pitch or catch, and whether they're active.
- **Lineups and playing time:** positions by inning, batting order, attendance, and season playing-time totals.
- **Team stats:** if a coach imports the team's GameChanger stats export, the season stats for each player. Only the team's coaches and managers can see these.
- **A player's own box score stats:** if a parent imports their child's box score from GameChanger, that game's line for their child only (hits, at-bats, pitching and so on). Only that child's linked parents can see these, not coaches and not other families. When a box score is uploaded, we read only that child's line and **do not keep the file**.
- **Walk-up songs and announcements:** a song a parent uploads or makes with AI, how to say the child's name and number, and the announcer recording. Coaches approve walk-ups, and anyone on the team can play approved walk-ups at a game.
- **Homework results:** which plays a family watched and quiz scores.

**We never collect a player's** email, phone number, home address, school, date of birth, photo, or anything that lets someone contact a child directly. RallyCap has **no private messaging**: team messages go to the team's group, never to an individual child.

### 3.3 Team information

Team name, age group, colors and logo; seasons; games and practices (dates, times, opponents, places and fields, results); tournaments (teams, schedules, scores, standings); uniforms; fair-play rules; practice plans; plays assigned as homework; team money (charges, payments, credits, expenses, receipts and who each charge is for); and the team's GroupMe connection.

### 3.4 Files and content you upload

- **Images, PDFs and spreadsheets** you upload so RallyCap can read them: schedules, uniform flyers, tournament screenshots, tie-break rules, box scores, and pictures of your team colors. We send these to our AI provider to read them. We don't keep the original file unless the feature says so.
- **Files we keep:** team and club logos; expense receipts, which are private to the team; and walk-up songs and team sounds, which are private to the team.

### 3.5 Payment information

Payments are handled by **Stripe**. **We never see or store your card number.** We receive and keep a record of what you bought, the amount, the date, and what it unlocked (for example a team's season, a club's codes, or player stats for your children).

### 3.6 Information collected automatically

- **Sign-in cookies.** We use cookies only to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies in the app.
- **On your device:** to work at the field with no signal, RallyCap saves a copy of your team's information and pages on your phone or computer (browser storage and a service worker), plus small preferences such as your last open team. Signing out clears it.
- **Server logs:** our hosting providers keep standard logs (such as IP address, browser type, and the pages and errors requested) for security, troubleshooting and abuse prevention.
- **Website analytics:** [We don't currently use analytics. If we add privacy-friendly analytics to our public website, we'll update this section first. We will not add session recording or advertising pixels inside the app.]

### 3.7 Location

When you add a game or practice location, you search for a place, and we store that place's name, address and map position. We **don't** track your device's location.

## 4. How we use information

We use information to:

1. Provide RallyCap. That covers building lineups, showing schedules and maps, posting to your team's GroupMe, tracking team money, running tournaments, and showing your child's stats.
2. Power features you choose to use. Examples: reading an uploaded schedule, box score or screenshot; suggesting practice plans; making a walk-up song or announcer call.
3. Keep accounts secure, prevent abuse, and enforce the rules about who can see what.
4. Process payments and unlock what you've paid for.
5. Send service messages, such as sign-in links, receipts, and notices about important changes. **We don't send marketing email without your consent**, and you can unsubscribe at any time.
6. Fix problems and improve RallyCap.

**We do not:**
- sell or rent personal information
- share it for targeted advertising
- build advertising profiles
- use children's information for marketing

**AI and your data:** we use AI providers (section 6) to process content for the features above. We send only what a feature needs. For example, AI practice plans send no player names, and a box score is read only to find your child's line. **We do not allow our AI providers to use your content to train their models** [confirm against each provider's current terms: commercial API terms generally exclude training].

## 5. Who can see what

RallyCap enforces these rules in its database, not just in the app:

| Who | Can see |
|---|---|
| **Everyone on the team** (anyone the coach invited) | The roster (names and numbers), games, practices, places, uniforms, approved walk-ups at a game, tournament schedules and standings, team money totals, and teammates' names and any phone numbers they chose to share |
| **Coaches and managers** | Also: lineups and the lineup tools, playing time, the team's imported GameChanger stats, parent–player links, and homework results |
| **Coaches and treasurers** | Team money in full (every entry and every family's balance) |
| **Parents** | Also: their own children's money charges, the team's expenses (Walkoffs), their own children's homework, and their own children's box score stats (if they've imported or unlocked them). Parents do **not** see other children's box score stats, other families' money details, or lineup tools |
| **A club's directors and admins** (only while the team holds that club's code) | View-only: the team's players, games, lineups, playing time, fair-play rules, imported team stats, and coaches' names. **Never** team money, parents or family links, walk-ups, GroupMe, or families' own box score stats |
| **RallyCap staff** | Only as needed to provide support, keep RallyCap secure, or comply with the law |

**Things you post to GroupMe** through RallyCap are sent to your team's GroupMe group, and from then on are governed by GroupMe's terms and privacy policy.

## 7. Children's privacy

RallyCap is meant for adults who run and support youth teams, and children do not create accounts. Information about players under 13 is provided by their coaches and parents or guardians to run the team. We collect only what's described in section 3.2, and we use it only to provide RallyCap to that team and family.

- **Coaches** confirm, when they add players, that they have the families' permission to add them (see our Terms).
- **Parents and guardians** can ask us to review, correct or delete their child's information at any time (section 9), even if they don't use RallyCap.
- If we learn that a child under 13 has created an account, or that we've collected a child's information in a way this policy doesn't allow, we'll delete it.

## 8. How long we keep information

- **Team information** stays while the team exists. Coaches can option (archive) a team and then delete it. Deleting a team deletes its roster, games, lineups, money, uniforms, walk-ups and other team content.
- **A player's own box score stats** belong to that child's family, not to a team. They stay available to the child's linked parents even if a team is deleted or a coach removes them. They're deleted when the parents delete them or close their accounts.
- **Your account** stays until you ask us to delete it.
- **Purchase records** are kept as long as needed for accounting and legal requirements, typically [7] years.
- **Uploaded files we only read** (schedules, box scores, screenshots) aren't kept after reading.
- **Backups and logs** are deleted on their regular cycle, typically within [30–90] days.

## 9. Your choices and rights

- **See and correct:** most of your information is visible and editable in the app (My profile, the roster, your child's page).
- **Remove optional details:** clear your phone number or Venmo handle at any time.
- **Leave a team or unlink a player:** both are in the app. Unlinking your child ends your access to their stats.
- **Export or delete:** email **[privacy@rallycap.team]** to get a copy of your information, or to delete your account or your child's information. We'll confirm your identity and respond within [30] days.
- **Parents and guardians** may make these requests for their children.
- **Marketing email:** unsubscribe at any time.

Depending on where you live (for example California or certain other U.S. states), you may have additional rights to know, access, correct, delete or port your information, and to appeal a decision. We honor those rights for everyone, and **we do not sell or share personal information** as those laws define it. You won't be treated differently for using your rights.

## 10. Security

We protect information with:
- encryption in transit (HTTPS)
- access rules enforced in the database, so each person sees only what their role allows
- secret keys kept on our servers only
- payments handled by Stripe

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects your information, we'll notify you as the law requires.
